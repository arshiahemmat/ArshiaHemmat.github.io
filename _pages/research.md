I have been fortunate to work across a broad spectrum of institutions, applying and extending multimodal AI techniques to a variety of problems.  Below you will find a chronological overview of my research roles and the key contributions made during each appointment.

### Video‑LLM Uncertainty Calibration and Training Data Extraction
**Torr Vision Group – University of Oxford** (Sept 2024 – Present) • *Supervisor: Prof. Philip Torr*

- Developed methodologies for calibrating uncertainty in video‑based vision‑language models.
- Generated comprehensive multiple‑choice question datasets from video using diffusion models to assess memorisation and generalisation.
- Ongoing work to be submitted to **NeurIPS 2025**.

### Vision‑Language Benchmark in the Educational Domain
**RIML – Sharif University of Technology** (Aug 2024 – Present) • *Supervisor: Prof. Mohammad Hossein Rohban*

- Designing a bilingual benchmark covering primary to high‑school curricula with rich metadata (difficulty, cognitive task, student success rate).
- Creating 7,500 Persian and 3,000 English multimodal multiple‑choice questions.
- Conducting zero‑shot, few‑shot and hallucination‑detection experiments on GPT‑4, Gemini and other vision‑language models.

### Shape vs. Scene Reasoning Benchmark
**Torr Vision Group – University of Oxford** (Mar 2024 – Aug 2024) • *Supervisor: Prof. Philip Torr*

- Led the development of a benchmark investigating whether vision‑language models rely on shape or scene context when recognising objects.
- Published as **Hidden in Plain Sight** at *NeurIPS 2024*, demonstrating major robustness gaps in current VLMs.

### 3‑D Vessel Segmentation and Counterfactual Modelling
**CAMP Chair – Technical University of Munich** (Sep 2023 – Mar 2024) • *Supervisor: Prof. Nassir Navab*

- Applied geometric deep learning and Fourier transforms to segment blood vessels in 3‑D medical imaging.
- Investigated counterfactual modelling using vision‑linguistic models like DRAGON to improve interpretability in medical domains.

### Robustness in Vision‑Language Models
**RIML – Sharif University of Technology** (Feb 2023 – Oct 2023) • *Supervisor: Prof. Mohammad Hossein Rohban*

- Analysed representation disentanglement in contrastive models such as CLIP and studied out‑of‑distribution behaviour and robustness.

### Zero‑Shot Learning in Medical Domain
**ILS Lab – University of Isfahan** (Aug 2021 – Nov 2022) • *Supervisor: Dr. Peyman Adibi*

- Leveraged manifold and geometric learning to bridge the gap between seen and unseen classes in the ChestX‑ray8 dataset.
- Demonstrated how zero‑shot approaches can reduce the reliance on large labelled medical datasets.
